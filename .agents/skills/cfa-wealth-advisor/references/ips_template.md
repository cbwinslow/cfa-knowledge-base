# Institutional Investment Policy Statement (IPS) Specification

Following CFA Level III Private Wealth Management standards:

## Section 1: Executive Summary & Client Profile
- Primary client & family beneficiaries
- Current investable asset base & tax jurisdictions

## Section 2: Return Objectives
- Real spending rate calculation: $\text{Spending Rate} = \frac{\text{Annual Distribution}}{\text{Portfolio Base}}$
- Inflation indexation (CPI / medical inflation)
- Real capital preservation & target bequest goals
- Pre-tax return conversion: $\text{Pre-Tax} = \frac{\text{After-Tax Required Return}}{1 - t}$

## Section 3: Risk Objectives
- **Ability to Take Risk** (Objective): Time horizon, wealth relative to liabilities, human capital stability, debt leverage.
- **Willingness to Take Risk** (Subjective): Behavioral profile, loss aversion, risk tolerance questionnaire.
- **Overall Risk Tolerance**: Constrained by $\min(\text{Ability}, \text{Willingness})$.

## Section 4: Constraints (TTLLU)
- **Time Horizon**: Single-stage vs. multi-stage (e.g. pre-retirement, active retirement, legacy).
- **Tax Considerations**: Capital gains vs. ordinary income tax rates, tax location (Taxable vs. Tax-Deferred vs. Tax-Exempt).
- **Liquidity Requirements**: Ongoing distributions, emergency buffer (6–12 months of expenses), upcoming capital commitments.
- **Legal & Regulatory**: Trust structures, power of attorney, ERISA restrictions.
- **Unique Circumstances**: ESG mandates, concentrated employer stock holdings, familial obligations.
