# CFA Level III Macroeconomic Stress-Testing & Scenario Rubrics

## 1. Scenario Formulation Principles

Under the CFA Institute Asset Allocation and Risk Management curriculum, stress testing serves as an essential complement to statistical models (like Mean-Variance Optimization and VaR), which frequently fail during non-normal fat-tailed market dislocations.

---

## 2. Core Macroeconomic Stress Scenarios

### A. The 1970s Stagflation Shock
* **Macro Driver**: Supply-side commodity shock with high wage-price spiral and rapid central bank interest rate hikes.
* **Asset Shocks**:
  * Global Equities: $-15\%$ to $-20\%$ (Multiple compression)
  * Fixed Income: $-10\%$ to $-15\%$ (Yields surge $+200$ to $+300$ bps)
  * Real Estate / Farmland: $+4\%$ to $+8\%$ (Direct inflation hedge)
  * Commodities / Gold: $+30\%$ to $+40\%$

### B. The 2008 Global Financial Crisis Liquidity Shock
* **Macro Driver**: Systemic banking crisis, private credit freeze, collapse in asset liquidity.
* **Asset Shocks**:
  * Global Equities: $-35\%$ to $-45\%$
  * High-Yield Credit: Spreads widen $+600$ to $+1000$ bps
  * US Treasuries (Flight to Safety): $+10\%$ to $+15\%$
  * Direct Real Estate: $-20\%$ to $-30\%$
  * Cash / T-Bills: Preserves $100\%$ capital value

### C. The 2022 Rapid Monetary Tightening Shock
* **Macro Driver**: Central banks aggressively hike policy rates to curb post-pandemic inflation, breaking the traditional negative stock-bond correlation ($\rho_{\text{stock, bond}} > 0$).
* **Asset Shocks**:
  * Global Equities: $-18\%$ to $-22\%$
  * Core Fixed Income: $-12\%$ to $-16\%$ (Long-duration assets heavily damaged)
  * Cash / SOFR: $+4.0\%$ to $+5.0\%$ annualized yield

### D. The Technological / AI Productivity Boom
* **Macro Driver**: Disinflationary productivity gains, surging corporate return on invested capital ($\text{ROIC}$), stable discount rates.
* **Asset Shocks**:
  * Large-Cap Global Equities: $+25\%$ to $+35\%$
  * Private Equity / Venture: $+20\%$ to $+30\%$
  * Fixed Income: $+3\%$ to $+5\%$ (Stable term structure)
